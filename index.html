A People’s Company, on the wings of ‘Innovation’ and ‘Trust’

RAY International Group, an established business house in the southeastern coastal land of the Arabian Peninsula, deeply embedded in the rich values of the Sultanate of Oman. A dynamic business corporation, leveraging on technology and innovation, sustainable industrial practices and eco-friendly energy-efficient systems while focusing on health and safety of its people and the environment.

Marking the beginning in the year 2005, RAY International Group has securely moored its presence in the region of Oman, in the areas of Energy and Technology, Engineering and Construction, Well and Wireline Services, Professional Training and Skill Development, Digital Solutions and lastly, Global Business Process Services.
